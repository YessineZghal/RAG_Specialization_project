# New Employee Onboarding FAQ

## When do I get my laptop?

IT ships your laptop to arrive on or before your start date. If it has not
arrived by 9 AM on day one, contact it-support@example.com.

## How do I set up my email?

Your company email account is created automatically two business days before
your start date. Login instructions are sent to the personal email address
you provided during the offer process.

## What is the probation period?

All new hires have a **90-day probation period**, during which either party
may end the employment relationship with two weeks' notice. Performance
check-ins happen at day 30, day 60, and day 90.

## How much paid time off do I get?

Full-time employees accrue **15 days** of paid time off (PTO) per year during
their first two years, increasing to 20 days after two years of tenure. PTO
accrues monthly and can be requested through the HR portal once accrued.

## Who is my manager?

Your manager is listed in the offer letter and in the HR system under "Direct
Reports." If this is unclear, ask in the #new-hires Slack channel.

## Is remote work allowed?

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing the
90-day probation period, subject to manager approval.
