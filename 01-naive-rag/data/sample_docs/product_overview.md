# Product Overview: Acme Notes

Acme Notes is a note-taking application for small teams, available on
desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and mobile (iOS, Android).

## Plans

- **Free** — up to 3 users, 1 GB storage, notes stored for 30 days of
  version history.
- **Team** — $8/user/month, unlimited users, 50 GB storage, unlimited
  version history, shared folders.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, and a
  dedicated account manager.

## Key features

- Real-time collaborative editing with up to 25 simultaneous editors per
  document.
- Offline mode: changes made offline sync automatically once the device
  reconnects.
- End-to-end encryption is available on the Team and Enterprise plans only;
  the Free plan uses encryption in transit and at rest, but not end-to-end.

## Data retention

Deleted notes remain recoverable from the trash for **30 days** before being
permanently erased. Enterprise customers can configure a custom retention
window between 7 and 365 days via the admin console.

## Support

Free plan users get community forum support. Team plan users get email
support with a 24-hour response time. Enterprise customers get a dedicated
Slack channel with a 4-hour response time during business hours.
